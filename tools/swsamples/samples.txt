# samples.txt - which recordings take the place of which sound of the game
# (tools/swsamples/build.py makes package/sounds from it).
#
# A line: NAME  LOUDNESS  LONGEST  source; source; ...
#   NAME      the request it replaces (docs/SOUNDS.md), or XWENG / XWEXT (the
#             X-wing's engine, from inside the cockpit / from outside: loops)
#   LOUDNESS  dB (full scale) of its loud part (speech: of its loudest 400 ms, so that
#             a line's reverb does not count), so that all fit together:
#             as loud as the board's own sound for the same thing, about
#             (its speech -29, its effects -37, its music -32), a little
#             louder where a recording has more to it
#   LONGEST   seconds it is cut to at most (faded out at the end); a loop:
#             its length, and where in the source it begins (LEN@START)
#   sources   several: one of them each time, at random (multisamples).
#             @sfx/ is the SW Ultimate SFX Collection, @voice/ the film
#             recordings in sounds/voice.
#
# Every sound is a recording from those two folders; nothing is made up.
